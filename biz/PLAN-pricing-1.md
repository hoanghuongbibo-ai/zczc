# Pricing video, part 1: Cold open + Setup (`assets/audio/pricing-1.mp3`, 209.2 s)

Page `biz/pricing-1.html` → `js/pricing1.js`, lip-sync `js/env-pricing-1.js`. Times are the narration's word times
(pocketsphinx, checked against the script). Fonts: Fredoka + Nunito (`clean`).

**Recurring cast:** **You** (skin tone, teal hoodie, short brown hair) and **Neighbour** (skin tone, yellow top, bob).
They compare screens through the whole video. Volunteers, shoppers and clerks are white-faced extras. Real people get
house-style cartoons from the supplied photos (none needed in part 1).

**Music:** `investigate` (new minor pizzicato bed) for the cold open. `bright` for the report and the city map,
`soft` for the "be really clear" Instacart-denial beat, and `investigate` again on "something else". About −20 dB under the voice.

**Guard rails:** Instacart is never shown using personal data. The FTC question is labelled "request for information —
not a finding". Every price is from the log. Illustrations with no data are tagged "illustration".
