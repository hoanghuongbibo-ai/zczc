## 6. Fact-Check Notes

**Calculated figures (not in any source; show math on screen or in description):**
- **~$280,000 "1990s-ratio house":** $87,599 (Redfin 2026 income) × 3.2 (JCHS 1990s ratio) = $280,317. Mixes sources and years; it's illustrative only.
- **Monthly payments $2,340 (6.1%) and $2,642 (7.28%):** $429,100 price, 10% down → $386,190 loan, 30-year fixed, principal and interest only. Difference ≈ $302/mo, ≈ $3,625/yr. Excludes taxes, insurance, PMI.
- **50-year mortgage:** same loan at 7.28%: payment ≈ $2,407 (−$235/mo); lifetime interest ≈ $1,057,871 vs ≈ $565,059 for a 30-year loan. Our math, consistent with Berner's "almost double." The FOX article's own example ($438,156 vs $816,396 on a "$500,000 home") doesn't reconcile with a $500K loan at 6.25%, so it was not used.
- **"Almost three-quarters" permit collapse:** (7.3 − 1.9) / 7.3 = 74%.
- **"Nearly triple the 2021 low":** 7.28 / 2.65 = 2.75.
- **"About a quarter of all mortgages below 3%":** 24.6% at the Q1 2022 peak (#13).

**Disputed or sensitive:**
- **The "40" figure.** It's real NAR data, but other sources disagree (#3, #4). The script attributes it and presents the comparison. It doesn't say NAR is lying. Note that NAR's mailed count differs between NAR's release (173,250) and the AEI critique (189,750). The script uses only the agreed 6,103 responses and 3.5% rate.
- **AEI vs. Wolf Street figures.** Wolf Street cites NY Fed CCP at 36.2 for 2025, but AEI's own headline and MBA's table say a median of 34. That may be mean vs. median. The script uses 34. Confirm in AEI's PDF.
- **Housing shortage.** Estimates conflict (3.7M–5.5M), and Cato disputes the method. The script presents the range plus the counterargument.
- **Institutional investors.** Ownership figures are the share of the *single-family rental stock*, not all homes. The script phrases it that way. Don't let the narration slip into "3% of homes."
- **Executive order.** It doesn't ban purchases. The script follows Cooley's description. **Open gap:** what agencies actually issued after Feb 2026, and whether any Senate bill passed.
- **50-year mortgage.** **Open gap:** whether FHFA or the GSEs ever implemented it. The script says only that it was "floated."
- **Fed hike, Sept 16, 2026.** Warsh quote fragments come from Central Banking's partial transcript. Verify the exact wording against the Fed press conference transcript before voiceover.
- **Redfin large-homes data** is from the 2022 ACS (published Jan 2024). It's older than the rest. Consider saying "as of 2022" on screen.
- **Under-35 homeownership.** HVS quarterly data is noisy. "Steepest decline of any age group" is NAHB's characterization.
- **SCF net worth.** NAHB rounds the figure to $396,000, and the Fed table shows $396,200. The script says "about $396,000."
- **Interpretive lines.** "The American Dream quietly turning into an inheritance" and "the real story is worse" are the narrator's interpretation, not sourced fact. They're framed as opinion in the ending.
