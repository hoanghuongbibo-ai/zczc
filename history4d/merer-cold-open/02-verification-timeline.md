# 02 — Verification list and timeline

**Status key:**
- **FACT**: established by direct evidence.
- **ESTIMATE**: a scholarly calculation.
- **DISPUTED**: interpretations differ.
- **RECONSTRUCTION**: artistic or dramatised detail.
- **MISSING EVIDENCE**: not supported, so don't assert it.

Sources are listed at the end. Wording checked during this session is marked ✔︎ checked. Anything that still needs a check against the printed edition is marked ☐ verify.
