### COLD OPEN
`[MUSIC: low strings, river ambience]`

**S01** *(no VO; 6 s of dawn mist over the flood)*

**S02** You wake up on the deck of a boat. It's July,
**S03** the air is already warm, and the river has swollen so wide you can barely see the far bank.
**S04** Forty men are sleeping around you, wrapped in linen.
**S05** Behind you rises a pale cliff of limestone with fresh chisel marks all over it.
**S06** And a few feet away, a man is already awake. He's holding a reed pen and a sheet of papyrus.
  **[SUGGEST: "He's holding a rush brush and a sheet of papyrus."]** Old Kingdom scribes wrote with a frayed rush brush.
**S07** He writes one line: where the team spent the night. Then he puts it away.
  `[SFX: brush scratch] [ON-SCREEN: "Spends the night at Tura South." — Papyrus Jarf B, trans. after Tallet]`
**S08** His name is Merer. He's your boss.
**S09** And today, like most days this summer, your job is to move a block of stone about as heavy as a pickup truck across this river to a building site 10 miles away.
  **[SUGGEST: "…your job is to move blocks of stone, each about as heavy as a pickup truck, across this river…"]** Each boatload carried many blocks, every few days.
**S10** Here's the strange part. That sheet of papyrus survived.
**S11** Four and a half thousand years later, archaeologists pulled it out of a cave on the Red Sea coast.
**S12** It's the oldest inscribed papyrus ever found. And it was written by a man who helped build the Great Pyramid.
  **[SUGGEST: "It's among the oldest inscribed papyri ever found…"]** or keep the line and subtitle it "found so far".
**S13** So forget the whips. Forget the aliens.
**S14** For the first time, we can ask the people who were actually there: what did the pyramid builders do all day? Who fed them? Who patched them up? And why did they do it at all?
**S15** The answer starts with that one line. Write it down.

### CHAPTER 1 — The world before: a king who needs a mountain
`[MUSIC: warm, curious]`
**S16** `[TITLE CARD]` To understand Merer's day, we first need to talk about his king.
**S17** `[MAP]` Khufu (KOO-foo) rules Egypt around 2,600 years before Jesus. Nobody agrees on his exact dates; one common estimate puts his reign at roughly 2589 to 2566 BC.
**S18** To his people, he isn't just a ruler. He's a god living on Earth, and when he dies, he has to make it safely to the sky.
**S19** That trip needs a launch pad. So Khufu orders the biggest building the world has ever seen: a pyramid about 481 feet tall, made of something like 2.3 million blocks.
  `[ON-SCREEN: "~481 ft tall (original) · ~2.3 million blocks (estimate)"]`
**S20** It will stay the tallest human-made structure on the planet for about 3,800 years.
**S21** `[TIMELINE]` To put that in time: Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the building of this pyramid.
**S22** Now, I know what you're thinking. Somebody had to do the actual work. And for about 2,500 years, the world's answer came from one Greek tourist.
  *(cliffhanger into chapter 2: Herodotus)*
