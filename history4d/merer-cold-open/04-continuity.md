# 04 — Character and environment continuity guide

**Rule:** every image and video prompt pastes the **exact descriptor string** in the code block. Don't paraphrase it, because changing even a few words causes drift. Generate a reference sheet for each character first (front, 3/4 and profile on a neutral background) and use it as the image or character reference in every later scene.

## Global look (append to every prompt)
```
GLOBAL_LOOK: cinematic photoreal historical documentary, Old Kingdom Egypt c. 2560 BC, natural light, anamorphic 35mm film look, fine film grain, shallow depth of field, volumetric haze, palette of ochre, limestone cream, Nile brown and linen white with small lapis-blue accents, no modern objects
```
- **Grade:** warm highlights, slightly teal shadows. Dawn scenes run rose-gold, midday bleached cream, archive scenes cool and neutral.
- **Light direction:** the sun rises from the east, over the Tura cliffs on the east bank, so in dawn scenes on the boat the light comes from behind the cliff and rakes across the deck.

## Characters

### MERER: inspector, the team leader (hero)
```
MERER: ancient Egyptian work-crew inspector named Merer, man about 35, lean wiry build, warm medium-brown skin, close-cropped black hair, clean-shaven, narrow oval face, calm watchful dark eyes, faint sun creases at the eyes; plain white knee-length linen kilt with a simple tied linen belt, bare chest, thin linen sash over the left shoulder; small wooden scribe palette with one red and one black ink cake, a thin frayed rush brush, a short roll of pale papyrus; barefoot
```
- **Never:** a reed pen or quill, jewellery beyond a simple cord, a beard, a wig in work scenes, or modern sandals.

### CREW: Merer's team (about 40 men)
```
CREW: Old Kingdom Egyptian work crew, men aged 18 to 40, sun-darkened brown skin, short-cropped black hair, lean muscular builds, plain off-white linen loincloths and short kilts, a few with simple linen headcloths, coils of palm-fibre rope, no metal armour, no whips
```

### KHUFU: the king (stylised only; no reliable likeness survives)
```
KHUFU: Pharaoh Khufu as a stylised royal figure, seated, striped gold-and-lapis-blue nemes headcloth, braided false royal beard, white pleated royal kilt, broad bead collar of gold and carnelian, face kept in deep shadow or turned away, statue-like stillness
```

### HERODOTUS: Greek writer, c. 430s BC (teaser only in S22)
```
HERODOTUS: Greek traveller and historian Herodotus, man about 50, full curly grey-brown beard, wool himation in faded blue over a cream chiton, leather sandals, holding a wax writing tablet and stylus, curious expression
```

### ARCHAEOLOGISTS: Wadi al-Jarf, 2013
```
ARCHAEOLOGISTS: modern archaeological team, mixed French and Egyptian men and women, sun hats, cotton field shirts, kneeling with brushes and trowels, archival tissue and trays, faces not identifiable
```
- Don't depict Pierre Tallet's real face. Keep everyone anonymous.

## Environments

### TURA: quarry bank, east bank of the Nile
```
TURA: Tura limestone quarries on the east bank of the Nile, a pale cream-white fine limestone cliff cut into stepped quarry faces with fresh copper-chisel marks and rectangular extraction bays, drifts of white limestone dust, date palms and acacia at the cliff foot, a mud landing bank with stacked dressed blocks
```

### BOAT: the cargo boat
```
BOAT: Old Kingdom Nile cargo boat about 20 metres long, plank-built of dark acacia and cedar wood, gently curved hull with upswept papyriform prow and stern, one large steering oar at the stern, bipod mast with a lowered rectangular linen sail, palm-fibre rope lashings, flat open deck loaded with several dressed limestone blocks on timber sledges
```

### NILE_FLOOD: the river in summer inundation
```
NILE_FLOOD: the Nile in its summer inundation, wide brown-silver floodwater spreading across the valley, the far bank a thin hazy line of palm groves, mist lying on the water at dawn
```

### GIZA_SITE: the building site seen across the river
```
GIZA_SITE: the Giza plateau building site c. 2560 BC on the west bank, a nearly finished pyramid with pale casing, rubble and mud-brick construction ramps, a harbour basin, worker camps with cooking smoke
```

### WADI_JARF: Red Sea coast, 2013 excavation
```
WADI_JARF: Wadi al-Jarf on Egypt's Red Sea coast, arid tan limestone hills, a row of rock-cut storage galleries partly sealed with large limestone blocks, wind-blown sand, turquoise sea and the line of an ancient stone jetty in the distance
```

### MUSEUM: papyrus display
```
MUSEUM: dim museum gallery, a single fragile papyrus fragment with black and red hieratic ink lying flat under low-reflection glass, soft cool spotlight, dark background
```

## Continuity checklist (per scene)
- Merer's kilt and sash: the sash is always over the **left** shoulder, and the palette is held in his left hand with the brush in his right.
- Blocks are 2–3-ton dressed limestone, the same cream colour as the TURA cliff.
- Crew count: about 40 bodies when the whole deck is visible.
- The boat's prow points **left** (downstream, north, toward Giza) in every boat shot, to keep screen direction.
- Water level and colour stay the same through S01–S09: brown-silver flood with mist that burns off by S09.
- Time of day: S01–S08 are dawn and S09 is early morning sun.
