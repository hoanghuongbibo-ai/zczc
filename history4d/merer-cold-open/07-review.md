## Visual consistency checklist (check each generated clip before editing)
- [ ] Merer: clean-shaven, close-cropped hair, white kilt, sash over the **left** shoulder, palette in his left hand and **rush brush** in his right (S06, S07, S08, S14-4, S15)
- [ ] Boat prow points screen-left in every boat shot (S01–S09, S15)
- [ ] Limestone is cream-white everywhere: no red granite at Tura, no sandstone
- [ ] No wheels, horses or camels; sledges, levers and ropes only
- [ ] No iron tools; copper only
- [ ] Water is brown-silver flood; mist burns off progressively from S01 to S09
- [ ] Pyramid has white casing in S09, S12, S19 and S22 (it was still largely intact in Herodotus' time); weathered only in the S20 time-lapse
- [ ] Hands: check finger counts in S06, S07, S14 and S22, and regenerate if wrong
- [ ] No generated text anywhere. All quotes, labels and titles are added in the edit
- [ ] Faces: the archaeologists can't be identified, and Khufu's face is never clear
