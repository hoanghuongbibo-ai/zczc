## Editing
1. **Assembly:** S01 to S22 in order. Lay the recorded VO first, then cut each scene's in-point to its first narrated word (the scene timecodes in 05 are targets; the final timing follows the real VO).
2. **Clip handling:**
   - Generate 2–3 takes per clip and pick the most consistent one.
   - Trim the first and last 0.5 s, where AI clips often warp.
   - Use speed ramps (90–110 %) to fit the VO, never above 115 %.
3. **Pacing:** don't hold any single image longer than 8 s. For longer scenes, cut between the A and B clips.
   - The cold open breathes slowly: S01 has no VO, and S10 and S13 each hold a beat of silence.
   - Chapter 1 is brisker.
4. **Depth enhancement:** for any clip with weak parallax, add a 2.5D layer pass (separate the FG, MG and BG with depth maps, then animate a 3–5 % push) plus real mist and dust overlays in screen blend.
5. **Audio mix:**
   - VO about −16 LUFS integrated, peaks −1.5 dBTP.
   - Music ducked to about −28 LUFS under the VO and about −20 in the gaps (S01, S10, S13 beat, S16).
   - SFX are sweetened with river, rope and brush foley.
   - Gentle 2-band compression on the VO, and a de-esser.
6. **Subtitles:**
   - Burned-in or SRT, 42 characters per line or fewer, 2 lines maximum, 1.2–6 s each.
   - White with a soft shadow, lower third.
   - Use the subtitle text in 06 (it includes "(so far)").
7. **On-screen text:**
   - Quote (S07), name card (S08), labels (S02 "Reconstruction", S11 "Wadi al-Jarf · 2013", S19 numbers, S22 "Herodotus · c. 430s BC"), chapter title (S16), timeline (S21).
   - Font: a serif for titles and quotes, a clean sans for labels.
8. **Colour grade:**
   - Unify all AI clips with one LUT: warm highlights, slightly teal shadows, controlled saturation.
   - Dawn runs rose-gold, archive scenes cool neutral, myths desaturated.
   - Add light 35 mm grain over everything to hide differences between generators.
9. **Transitions:** use the ones listed per scene in 06. Avoid stock wipes; prefer match cuts, mist wipes and light flares.
10. **Export:** 3840×2160 or 1920×1080, 24 fps, H.264 high or ProRes master, AAC 320 kbps.
