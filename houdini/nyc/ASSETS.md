## Make it pop
- **One fixed camera, many eras.** The corner of Wall and Broad returns in every era from the same angle. The background changes (1609 marsh, 1653 wall, 1776 night fire, 1835 frozen fire, 1931 skyline, 1975 grey, 2020 empty, today) and only a lamppost or rock stays put as the anchor.
- **The obituary counter.** Each time, a black-bordered "NEW YORK IS FINISHED" notice slams down and the count ticks up. A population ticker then jumps past the previous record, and the joke is that every obituary is followed by a bigger number. The counter stays off in Beat 8, as written.
- **The Harbor as a character.** A water character in the same family as the Venice lagoon, "the one character who never leaves". It reacts in every beat and gets the last shot. I'll send a character sheet for approval before the build.

## Fact-check issues (no source log received)
