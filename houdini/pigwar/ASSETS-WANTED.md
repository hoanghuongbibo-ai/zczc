# The Pig War — real-life assets wanted

Drop files into `houdini/pigwar/assets/img/` (photos) or `houdini/pigwar/assets/audio/` (sound), using the file names below.
Every photo is shown as a framed insert (an old-photo frame, a pinned print or a map card), at most a few seconds, only where the narration
names that person, place or document. Anything you don't send simply stays drawn.

**Format:** JPG or PNG, at least 1600 px on the long side, public domain or licensed for YouTube. Scans of old prints are fine.
Send the source/credit line with each file so it can go in the description.

## 6. Sound

Bird chirps, gull calls, whistles and the siren-like riser are now removed from the Pig War mix. If you want your replacement sounds
inside the film (instead of adding them in your editor), send them as WAV or MP3 and tell me where they go:

- `sfx-bigger.wav` — the "SOMEBODY, SOMEWHERE… BIGGER" lever gag (6:45, 7:45)
- `sfx-shot.wav` — the single shot that kills the pig (1:12, 16:38)
- `amb-meadow.wav` — a calm outdoor bed with no birds (island scenes)
- `amb-sea.wav` — water lapping / harbour without gulls (bay and ship scenes)
- `sfx-flag.wav`, `sfx-ship-bell.wav`, `sfx-cannon.wav` — optional upgrades of the synthesised ones
