- Newspapers and mastheads are made up. "NFL" is only spoken; the stadium has no logos.
- Music: synthesised beds per beat — light pluck (setup), market (town life), tension (Sulla, eruption build), single low drone (from "In Pompeii itself…" to the twist), gentle (casts), brass newsreel (1943), warm (ending).
