# Who Owns the Game? Tencent story animation

**Status:** the full cut is built. Open `index.html`, or watch `out/tencent-story.mp4` (59.9 s, 1280×720, 30 fps).
`preview.html` is the 14 s vintage preview and `preview-doodle.html` is the first doodle-style preview.

## Look

A vintage print-ad collage, matched to the user's reference clip:

- **Print texture:** halftone dot printing with slightly misaligned inks, aged paper, film grain and a slight frame jitter.
- **Editing:** hard cuts on the narration's pauses, with a slow push-in or drift on every shot.
- **Text:** pasted word strips in retro italic and slab type, plus fake newsprint.
- **Palette:** mustard, cream, brick red, near-black, teal and navy.

All artwork is original and drawn in code: invented figures, props and type layouts. Studio names appear only as text labels, with no logos. The music is an original procedural jazz bed.

## Constraints and decisions

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Repo assets | The repo was empty, so every visual is generated in code. No image files are used. |
| `voice_1.mp3` (15m 12s, the full script) | The cut uses 11 excerpts, 59.9 s in total. They were located with a pocketsphinx word-level transcript plus ffmpeg silence detection; boundaries are listed in `tools/build-narration.py`. |
| Length | Kept under 60 s. Cut beats: the "copy" line, "Pony / Ma = horse", "Their leader was Ma Huateng", Fortnite and "spiritual opium". |
| Tencent mascot | Not used. Tencent appears as a plain red "TENCENT" tag. |

## Tech (pure JavaScript, zero runtime dependencies)

- `js/engine.js` holds the core canvas helpers (paper, ink, stamps, ransom lettering) and the frame renderer.
- `js/print.js` provides the halftone plates, misaligned inks, newsprint, word strips, grain and jitter, the shot sequencer, and the original bust, horse and skyline drawings.
- `js/props.js` has the monitor, globe, pie, burst, pentagon, gamepad, hands, scissors, cheques, boxes, hanging tags and the self-drawing chart.
- `js/sound.js` synthesises the music and effects: a jazz bed (walking bass, brushes, piano comping, vibraphone, vinyl crackle) and paper slap and thud effects.
- `js/timing.js` is generated by `tools/build-narration.py` and holds each excerpt's start time, length and pauses, so cuts follow the voice.
- `js/show-vintage.js` defines the full shot list. `js/player.js` keeps playback in sync and supports `?t=` stills.
- `tools/export-video.mjs` renders frames deterministically in headless Chromium and uses ffmpeg to produce the MP4.

Rebuild:

```
python3 tools/build-narration.py path/to/voice_1.mp3   # narration-full.mp3 + js/timing.js
node tools/export-video.mjs index.html out/tencent-story.mp4 30
```
